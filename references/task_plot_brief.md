# Task Plot Brief

## Task

- Title: Trier Social Stress Test
- Construct: psychosocial stress induction / social-evaluative threat
- Paradigm: fixed-script TSST with baseline, preparation, speech, mental arithmetic, and recovery.

## Rows

- Baseline and preparation: instruction, baseline acclimation, and speech preparation.
- Speech stress: public speech with judges and recording indicator.
- Arithmetic and recovery: serial subtraction with judges, then quiet recovery and goodbye.

## Protocol Timeline

1. Instruction: 100 ms hold, space to start.
2. Baseline acclimation: 300000 ms quiet fixation.
3. Speech preparation: 600000 ms prepare a job-fit speech.
4. Speech delivery: 300000 ms speech under neutral judges and REC cue.
5. Mental arithmetic: 300000 ms count backward from 2043 by 17 under observation.
6. Recovery: 900000 ms quiet rest with fixation.
7. Goodbye: 100 ms hold, space to exit.

## Notes

- There are no points, rewards, accuracy scoring, or adaptive staircase.
- Only space starts instruction and dismisses the goodbye screen.
- QA/sim shorten the same phase sequence, but the human task uses the canonical long durations above.
